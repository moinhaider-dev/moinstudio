Hatbox privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy covers Hatbox, a packing puzzle game for Android made by Moin Studio. It describes how the app actually works.

The short version

Hatbox doesn't collect your personal data. There are no accounts. In the free part of the game you can choose to watch an ad to get a hint; the full game has no ads.

What stays on your phone

Your puzzle progress, best scores, streak and settings are saved only in Hatbox's own storage on your phone. We can't see them.

Ads (only if you choose to watch one)

Hatbox uses Google AdMob to show a short ad when a player taps "watch ad" for an extra hint. Ads never appear on their own.

  • Every ad request is marked as directed at children and follows Google's rules for apps used by children.
  • Ads are limited to content rated for all ages (G) and are never personalised.
  • Hatbox does not use your device's advertising ID.
  • Google may collect basic device information, such as the device model and IP address, to show the ad and count that it was seen. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • In the UK and EEA you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy & ads choices.

Unlocking the full game removes all ads.

Payments

The full game is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment and we never see your card details. Google only tells Hatbox whether your account owns the full game.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is created on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Feedback

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

Hatbox is made for everyone, children included. It collects no personal information from anyone, has no accounts, chat or links to social media, and its optional ads follow Google's Families rules.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Reset progress, or uninstall Hatbox. Your full game purchase stays with your Google account and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email moinstudio.support@gmail.com.
