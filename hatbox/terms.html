You can play Chapter 1 and the daily box for free. Please don't copy or resell the game or its pictures.

The full game

The full game is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It unlocks every chapter, past daily boxes, unlimited hints and no ads. Refunds follow Google Play's refund rules.

Ads

Ads are optional and only appear when you choose to watch one for a hint. The full game has no ads.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your phone. If you delete the app or reset progress, it can't be brought back. Your purchase can always be restored with the same Google account.
