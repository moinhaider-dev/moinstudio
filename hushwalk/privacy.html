Hushwalk privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy covers Hushwalk, a stealth puzzle game for Android made by Moin Studio. It describes how the app actually works.

The short version

Hushwalk has no accounts. Your progress, settings and purchase status stay on your phone. If ads are on, Google AdMob shows them.

What stays on your phone

Your floors, goals, best times, ratings and settings are saved only in Hushwalk's own storage on your phone. We can't see them.

Ads

Hushwalk uses Google AdMob in two ways:

  • Optional ads: when you tap Rewind, Peek or +1 coin, a short ad plays. Nothing plays unless you tap.
  • Ad breaks: from floor 8, a short ad between floors, at most once every 3 cleared floors and never right after you're caught.

AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. Google may also collect basic device information, such as the device model and IP address. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the UK and EEA you choose this first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Purchases

Google Play handles the Remove ads purchase. We never see your payment details. Google only tells Hushwalk whether your account owns Remove ads.

Crash reports

None in version 1. If we add them we will say so here first.

Feedback

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

Hushwalk is not made for children under 13 and asks for no personal details.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Reset all progress, or uninstall Hushwalk. A Remove ads purchase stays with your Google account and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email moinstudio.support@gmail.com.
