Boli terms of use

Last updated 17 November 2026

Use Boli to learn, and cancel Premium whenever you like. We work hard on accuracy, but Urdu varies between families and regions.

Free and Premium

The script lessons, Family units 1–2 and the first GCSE theme are free, with no time limit and no ads. Premium unlocks everything else.

Subscriptions and trials

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically through Google Play until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in your Google Play account. A free trial turns into a paid plan unless you cancel before it ends.

Lifetime

Lifetime is a one-time payment for Premium for as long as Boli is offered, including future lessons.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund rules. We can't issue them directly.

About the content

We check every phrase carefully, but spoken Urdu differs between families and regions. If a phrase isn’t how your family says it, tell us from the Phrasebook. GCSE content follows the published AQA themes. Boli is not made, endorsed or checked by AQA or any exam board.

Your recordings

Recordings you make are yours and stay on your phone.
