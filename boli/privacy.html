Boli privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy covers Boli: Family & GCSE Urdu, an Android app made by Moin Studio. It describes how the app actually works.

The short version

Boli has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't send your learning data anywhere. Your progress stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, Review schedule, saved phrases and settings. They are stored only on your phone. We can't see them.

Your microphone

Boli uses the microphone only while you're recording, after you tap Record, so you can hear how you sound. Your latest recording of each phrase is saved on your phone only. It is never uploaded, and Delete all data removes it.

Notifications

Only the one daily reminder you choose to turn on. You can change the time or turn it off in Me.

Payments

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We only learn whether Premium is active, never your card details.

Backups

"Export progress" makes a file on your phone. You decide where it goes. "Import progress" reads a file you pick.

Feedback

"Send feedback" opens your own email app. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply and improve the app.

Analytics and tracking

Boli has no analytics, no advertising IDs and no tracking. Google Play may show us anonymous download and crash numbers under its own policies.

Who Boli is for

Boli is made for teenagers (13 and over) and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me, then Delete all data, or uninstall Boli. Backup files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app before the change applies.

Contact

Questions? Email moinstudio.support@gmail.com.
