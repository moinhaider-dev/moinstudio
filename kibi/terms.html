Kibi terms of use

Last updated 7 November 2026

What Kibi is

A tool to record your pets' care and remind you when it's due. It does not give veterinary advice. Always ask your vet about your pet's health.

Reminders

Reminders rely on your phone. If notifications are off, or a battery saver stops them, a reminder may not arrive. Please don't rely on Kibi alone for urgent or critical medicine.

Free and Premium

Free covers 2 pets with schedules, reminders and records. Premium adds unlimited pets, the health log and vet reports. Your free features stay free if you never upgrade.

Paying

Premium is a subscription, monthly or yearly, that renews until you cancel it in Google Play. The yearly plan may start with a 7-day free trial; Google Play shows whether you can have it before you pay. Refunds follow Google Play's rules.

Your data

Your data is yours and stays on your phone. We can't see it, so we can't recover it if your phone is lost. Export a backup now and then.

Our promise

We work hard to keep Kibi reliable and honest, but like any app it can have bugs. Tell us and we'll fix them.
