Kibi privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy covers Kibi: Pet Care Reminders, an Android app made by Moin Studio. It describes how the app actually works.

The short version

Kibi keeps your pets' information on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no Kibi servers holding your data.

What Kibi stores

The pets, care tasks, reminders, health notes, photos and records you add. They are saved only in Kibi's own storage on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it: exporting a backup file, sharing a vet report, or emailing us feedback. You decide where those go.

Payments

Kibi Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and we never see your card details. Google only tells Kibi whether your account has Premium.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: used only for the care reminders you set.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you add a photo, and only for the photo you pick. Photos stay on your phone.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete all data, or uninstall Kibi. Backup files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Who Kibi is for

Kibi is made for adults looking after pets. It is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and the app will show the new version.
