Tendril privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy covers Tendril: Plant Care Reminders, an Android app made by Moin Studio. It describes how the app actually works.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your phone. Your plants, care history, photos, journal notes and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

What we never do

No ads, no tracking and no analytics. We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Where your data lives

On your phone only. If you delete the app or use “Delete all my data” in the app, it's gone. Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone too.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show your daily care reminders.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to add a photo of a plant. Photos stay on your phone.

Payments

Tendril Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see them.

Your choices

You can export everything or delete all your data at any time from the Profile tab in the app.

Children

Tendril is a general plant care app and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app first if the change matters.

Contact

Questions? Email moinstudio.support@gmail.com.
